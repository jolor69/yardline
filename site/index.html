Questions, answered plainly

How does matching actually work?
You create a profile as a buyer or seller and set your criteria — equipment type, spec, budget or price, and location. Each day we check active buyer and seller criteria against each other. When there’s a match, our team reviews it — once approved, we share contact details so you can talk directly.

How are buyers and sellers verified?
Before an account can send or receive a match, we confirm the business behind it — company registration and a working phone number, at minimum. We don’t match anonymous accounts.

Can I look around before I subscribe?
Yes — the equipment categories we cover are visible without an account. Setting criteria and receiving a match requires an active buyer or seller subscription.

What happens after we’re matched?
Once our team reviews and approves the match, we share contact details for both sides. From there, inspection and pricing are between you and the other party. If the match leads to a completed sale, a commission applies at a rate set by Yardline — see Pricing for details.

Which regions do you cover?
Coverage depends on how many buyers and sellers are active in a region — matching gets better as more accounts join nearby. Set a location radius when you create your profile and we’ll only match within it.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account settings — you’ll keep matching until the end of the period you’ve already paid for, then it stops. No retention call required.

Is there a commission on top of the subscription?
Yes. The subscription covers access to matching; when a match closes into a completed sale, a commission on the sale price applies too, at a rate set by Yardline and shown before you subscribe.

Does a subscription guarantee I’ll get matched or make a sale?
No. A subscription gives you access to the matching system, and commission only applies if a sale actually happens — neither a match, a sale, nor a purchase is guaranteed.
